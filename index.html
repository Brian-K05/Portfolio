Hey! I'm Brian Kyle Salor, a 4th year BSIT student at Leyte Normal University, currently based in Tacloban City. Originally from San Isidro, Northern Samar, I'm passionate about web development, training AI, and building custom AI solutions. What drives me is curiosity—when I see problems in tech, I can't help but explore what's possible to build.

My coding journey started with Java in my first year, then Python (which I fell in love with), followed by Visual Basic. I'm also deeply interested in AI tools and leverage them to make my work faster and more efficient. As a backend developer and project lead for our capstone mobile app, I've learned the importance of both technical skills and team leadership.
